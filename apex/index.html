About

I’m a product designer and inventor. Day to day, I run Loamist, a Berkeley climate-tech company building infrastructure for biomass-based carbon removal (Breakthrough Energy Fellow, cohort 4).

Before that I led innovation at The Flex Company, co-founded Keela — the best-selling menstrual cup in the United States by unit volume — and worked at the intersection of bioengineering, medical devices, and consumer product design. Sixteen patents, work in the permanent collection of the Cooper Hewitt Smithsonian Design Museum, and a handful of awards listed below.

On the side I help friends, neighbors, and small businesses get simple, well-built websites up. If that’s you, say hi.
